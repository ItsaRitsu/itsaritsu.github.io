I alter sleeves, collars, waistlines, crotches and pant legs, set invisible zippers and buttonholes, and I'm comfortable on a machine or a serger. Below are five projects with the process included — the pattern drafts and design iterations, not just the finished pieces.

01

CUSTOM ESTHETICIAN APRONS

2026 — Present

Salon stylists and estheticians stand in these for ten hours, so I start by asking what they actually carry and where their hands go. Then I measure the wearer, draft the pattern, and build to that spec: pocket placement, adjustable straps, washable fabric that survives color and product.

I sketched out the waistband length, gathered skirt width, a 4 × 37 in ruffle, 6 × 9 in patch pockets cut 7 × 10 for seam allowance, and a shaped sleeve ruffle custom to each client.. Each apron also gets a bespoke, personal design element- lace, appliqué or a patch.

SKILLS DEMONSTRATED
Measuring a client Pattern drafting to measurement Waistband construction Gathering & ruffle hems Patch pockets Pressing & finishing Client consultation to spec
02

CUSTOM PERFORMANCE WEAR

Professional string trio, 2020

A coordinated three-garment set for a working chamber ensemble. I developed one color-blocked design and applied it across three individual fits, so the trio reads as a set on stage while each dress is cut to its own wearer.

I sourced coordinating knits and engineered range of motion for shoulder mobility for bowing as well as seated cello playing.

SKILLS DEMONSTRATED
Fitting three different bodies One design across multiple sizes Sewing knits Color-blocked panel construction Engineering range of motion
03

REWORKED DENIM JACKET

Upcycle commission

A secondhand jacket the client loved but never wore. I unpicked the yokes and the upper back, cut new panels in embroidered mesh, and set them into the original seam lines so the topstitching still reads as the jacket's own.

Sheer mesh on heavy denim is the hard part: two fabrics with nothing in common at one seam. It's stabilised, stitched from the denim side, and the raw mesh edge is left deliberately frayed. Sleeves were re-set and the fit taken in at the shoulder.

SKILLS DEMONSTRATED
Alterations to an existing garment Panel replacement Seam ripping & rebuilding Setting sleeves Taking in at the shoulder Sheer & unstable fabrics Matching existing topstitching
04

HARLEQUIN JACKET

Costume commission

Drafted from the wearer's recorded measurements and built as a diamond patchwork — every point matched by hand before the panels were joined, then trimmed in gold braid along each seam.

The satin collar is interfaced and set with a clean roll line; the back yoke carries a beaded trim applied one drop at a time. Fully lined, hemmed by hand, and fitted through two rounds of revisions.

SKILLS DEMONSTRATED
Drafting from recorded measurements Fittings & revisions Precision piecing Collar setting & interfacing Lining Trim & bead application Hand finishing
05

BRIDAL VEIL — DESIGN EXPLORATION

Private client

For a client who's not sure what she wants, I draw options: several lengths, feather trim, lettuce hem, layered tulle, silk ruffles withi pinned reference photos beside them, and we talked through veil-forward, veil-back and chest height until one direction prevails.

The finished veil is the feather variation, hemmed by machine on tulle and trimmed by hand.

SKILLS DEMONSTRATED
Consulting a customer on options Design iteration Hem finishes — lettuce, rolled, machine Tulle, chiffon & silk Feather & trim application
